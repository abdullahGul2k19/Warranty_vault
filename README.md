# Warranty & Replacement Tracker

A simple purchase tracking application that helps users manage product warranties and replacement deadlines. It keeps purchase details and receipts organized and sends reminders before replacement or warranty periods expire.

## Features

- **Purchase Tracking:** Save item name, shop, price, and purchase date.
- **Receipt Storage:** Upload receipt and warranty card images.
- **Deadline Tracking:** Monitor replacement and warranty expiry dates.
- **Notifications:** Get reminders before deadlines expire.
- **Claim Pack:** View purchase details, shop information, and receipt together for easy warranty claims.

## Problem It Solves

Many shops in Pakistan offer limited replacement periods and short warranties on electronic products. People often forget expiry dates or lose their receipts, making it difficult to claim replacements or warranties.

This application provides a simple solution by keeping all purchase records in one place and reminding users before their deadlines expire.

## Target Users

- University students
- Laptop and mobile phone users
- People purchasing electronic accessories
- Anyone who wants to keep track of product warranties

## Project Goal

To make warranty management easier by helping users organize purchase records, save receipts, and avoid missing important replacement and warranty deadlines.

## Future Improvements

- Email and push notifications
- Export warranty records as PDF
